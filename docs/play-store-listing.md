## English listing copy

**App name:** ChooChooTracker

**Short description:** Multi-engine music tracker and groovebox for Android.

**Full description:**

ChooChooTracker is a fast, LSDJ-inspired music tracker with a broad palette of synthesis engines, PCM samples, wavetables, effects, and WAV export. Compose complete songs with a gamepad, hardware keyboard, or responsive on-screen controls.

Designed for phones and tablets, ChooChooTracker works in portrait and landscape. When no controller is connected, it provides a virtual D-pad and action buttons. Projects and samples stay on your device; use Android's document picker to import files and save exports where you choose.
